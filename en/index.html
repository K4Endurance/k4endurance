For runners who want more than more miles.

More is better? In trail running, that is often the direct route to overload. Intelligent training does not require 100-mile volume to reach your goal. It is about the quality of the stimulus, not the sum of your steps.

  • Scientific training guidance instead of guesswork
  • A focus on injury prevention and longevity
  • Training adapted to real life

What “better” really means.

monitoring

Measurable precision

We look at VO2max, lactate thresholds and running economy. Your progress becomes measurable, not merely a feeling.

psychology

Mental resilience

Trail running is 70% mental. We develop strategies for the difficult moments of ultras and technical terrain.

mountain_flag

Your summit

For one athlete, it is a first finish; for another, an FKT or podium. Together, we define what better means for you.

Why this approach works.

Pragmatism beats dogma. My methods draw on current sports science and are applied to the unpredictable reality of the mountains.

01

Evidence-based

Every session has a purpose. We use proven models for intensity distribution to get the most out of your time.

02

Health first

An injured runner does not improve. We prioritise tendon and joint health through strength training and smart tapering.

03

Practical utility

The mountains do not forgive theoretical mistakes. Training is practical, from downhill skills to fuelling under load.

04

Closed loop

Feedback is not a one-way street. We refine your plan every week using data and subjective feedback.

Why this approach works.

Pragmatism over dogma. My methods are grounded in current sports science – applied to the unpredictable reality of the mountains.

01

Every session has a purpose

No session without a goal. Intensity and volume are distributed deliberately so your training time has maximum impact.

02

Health first

An injured runner doesn’t get better. Targeted strength training and smart load management make tendons and joints resilient.

03

Practice over theory

The mountains don’t forgive theoretical mistakes. Downhill technique, poles, fueling under fatigue: we train what counts on race day.

04

Continuous adjustment

Your life changes, so does your plan. Your feedback and data feed into the planning on a regular basis.
